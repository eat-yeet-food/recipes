- Large mixing bowl

## Steps

### Levain

1. Mix starter with water and flour
2. Cover and rest at 78°F for 5h

### Autolyse

1. Mix flour and the first portion of water (290g) until shaggy
2. Pinch in the second portion of water (15g) until dry bits and chunks are gone
3. Cover and rest at 78°F for 3h. Add levain to autolyse dough and mix until just combined (fermentalyse) + cover and rest at 78°F for 20m

### Bulk Fermentation

1. Gently pinch in salt + remainder of water (15g). Rubaud and stretch in the bowl for 8m + cover and rest at 78°F for 20m (30m elapsed in bulk)
2. Strong fold + cover and rest at 78°F for 30m (1:00h elapsed in bulk)
3. Strong fold + cover and rest at 78°F for 30m (1:30h elapsed in bulk)
4. Coil fold + cover and rest at 78°F for 30m (2:00h elapsed in bulk)
5. Coil fold + cover and rest at 78°F for approximately 2:00h (2:30h elapsed in bulk)
6. End of bulk when risen approximately 50% in volume (approximately 4:30h elapsed in bulk)

### Shape

1. Preshape and rest at 70°F for 30m
2. Shape and place in banneton and rest at 70°F for 30m
3. Place in 38°F refrigerator for 12-18h

### Bake

1. Preheat oven at 550°F for 30m
2. Score loaf at 45° down center and bake in a covered dutch oven at 500°F for 6m
3. Reinforcement expansion score, cover and bake for 14m
4. Bake uncovered at 500°F for 25m
5. Transfer to cooling rack and cool for 2h before cutting

## Notes

- The autolyse process allows flour to fully hydrate and develops gluten naturally
- Bulk fermentation timing can vary based on temperature and starter activity
- Cold retarding in the refrigerator develops flavor and makes shaping easier
- Proper scoring allows controlled expansion during baking
- High initial heat creates steam for crust development

## Tips

- Keep your starter healthy and active for best results
- Temperature control is crucial throughout the process
- Don't rush the fermentation process - time develops flavor
- Use a kitchen scale for accurate measurements
- Watch [stretch and fold technique](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/how-to-stretch-and-fold-sourdough-bread-dough/) for proper bulk fermentation
- Master [preshaping technique](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/guides/how-to-preshape-bread-dough/) for better final shape
- Learn [batard shaping technique](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/guides/shaping-a-batard/) for oval-shaped loaves
- Learn proper [scoring techniques](https://www.theperfectloaf.com/guides/the-ultimate-guide-to-baking-bread-dough/) for optimal oven spring
- Try the [6-minute reinforcement score technique](https://www.tiktok.com/@eatbasilandbloom/video/7455793769011514654) for better ear development
- Read about [percent rise](https://thesourdoughjourney.com/the-mystery-of-percentage-rise-in-bulk-fermentation/) to help understand when bulk fermentation is complete
