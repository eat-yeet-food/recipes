## Ingredients

- 2 sticks unsalted butter (65-68°F)
- 454g powdered sugar (sifted)
- 2 tbsp heavy cream
- ⅛ tsp fine sea salt
- 1 tbsp [vanilla extract](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=nielsen+massey+vanilla+extract)

## Steps

1. Whip the butter: In a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat the butter on low speed, then increase to medium-high speed and whip for about 3 minutes until light and smooth. Scrape down the sides and bottom of the bowl.
2. Add powdered sugar: Add the powdered sugar in three additions, mixing on low speed between each addition. Scrape down the bowl after each addition.
3. Add remaining ingredients: Add the vanilla extract, salt, and heavy cream.
4. Final whip: Beat on high speed for 1 minute until smooth, fluffy, and fully combined.
