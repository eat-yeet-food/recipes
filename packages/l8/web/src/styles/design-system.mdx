# Eat / Yeet: plum + pink

Deep plum ink, bubblegum pink, an orange accent, real food on white. Avenir carries reading, Geller headings, Bowlby the brand. The normative contract is **docs/design-system.md** in the repository; this handbook shows the production components that implement it. If the two ever disagree, the doc wins and the handbook is a bug.

## Foundations

- **Color**: role tokens, the plum ramp, and the one selected look (pink fill, plum label).
- **Typography**: the named type scale and two uppercase letter-spacings.
- **Spacing & Radius**: five radii by role; nested radii are outer minus inset.
- **Elevation & Motion**: card, raised, overlay.
- **Gradients & Accents**: the plum hero gradient, and the pink→orange accent used only as the **stripe** (top of an action card or dialog, one per view) and the **accent rule** (under a title).
- **Iconography**: Lucide in currentColor; the pink-icing doughnut favicon.

## The rules that come up most

- **Fields** are light (white, 1px edge, plum text) in dialogs and cards, or `underline` on page forms. There are no dark fields, and no fields on solid pink or plum.
- **Tint** is only for informational asides and History's current row. Controls sit on white.
- **Selected** is always pink with a plum label: Choice Group, Toggle, the highlighted search result.
- **Pink is never text on white.** Secondary text is `muted-foreground`, never transparent ink.

## Where to look

**Primitives** are the owned controls (Button, Toggle, Fields, Choice Group, Dialog…). **Patterns** compose them (Surface, Accent Rule, Typography, Breadcrumbs, Callout, Disclosure, Catalog Cards, Content Blocks). **Shell** is navigation, account and error states. **Features** are Home, Recipes, Learn, Search and Notebook; **Pages** are full compositions.

Home and Notebook page stories live in `packages/l8/web/src/stories` because they compose the active app's configuration and registries (`@app/*`), which only web composition may import. Every other story lives beside its component.

Every component page opens with when to use it, when not to, and its variants by role. `pnpm test:storybook` renders every story with axe and play functions; it does not replace keyboard, visual or screen-reader review.
